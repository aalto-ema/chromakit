# Changelog

## Unreleased — 0.1.0 development

Initial public source preparation; no packaged stable release has been published.

- PySide6 desktop workspace and root `run_app.py` launcher.
- Reviewed CSV/TSV/TXT mapping, locale/encoding controls, immutable samples and units.
- Explicit omission of missing signal entries with gap and source-line provenance.
- Whole-trace arPLS/AsLS and manual baseline correction, refinement and live preview.
- Signed peak detection/integration, editable bounds, measurements and undo/redo.
- Gaussian/EMG overlapping-peak fits, editable constraints/locks, live residuals,
  component areas, count comparisons, fit diagnostics and accepted history.
- Synthetic examples, scientific documentation and numerical/Qt tests.
- MIT license, contributor/security guidance, issue/PR templates and public CI.

### Workflow expansion

- Portable projects with embedded data/images, accepted results/drafts/history and atomic saves.
- PNG/TIFF/PDF/SVG figures with dimensions/transparency/fonts and CSV/JSON results.
- Editable compound labels with review state, placement and stale-assignment protection.
- Calibrated PNG/JPEG/TIFF image tracing with masks, manual corrections and explicit gaps.
- Optional bounded gap interpolation and isolated-spike repair with live original/prepared
  comparison and preserved original pixel points/settings for reversible image refinement.
- External-standard linear calibration, weighting/intercept options and ranged concentrations.
- Text batches with reusable active recipes, individual outcomes and cancellation.
- Project/CSV/JSON compound libraries with reviewed ambiguous retention matches.

Native vendor readers and advanced workflow refinements remain planned.

### CI compatibility

- Constrain PySide6 below 6.12 following a repeatable Linux/Python 3.11 interpreter
  crash during post-test Qt cleanup with 6.12.0. Keep the full Python 3.11 test job
  and normal process-exit checks enabled.

### Comparison, image recovery and fit stability

- Read-only raw/corrected overlays, colors, explicit normalization, gap-aware reference
  differences, saved comparison settings and physical-size figure/CSV export.
- Arbitrary deskew, four-corner perspective rectification, per-column recovery flags
  and reviewed multi-color trace collections with preserved image/geometry provenance.
- Repeated-start fit sensitivity with constraints/locks retained, matched component-area
  ranges, per-attempt curves/residuals, cancellation and saved/exportable reports.
